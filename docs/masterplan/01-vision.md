# Chapter 1 — Vision

> **Positioning update (founder, 2026-09-30):** Rivaly is **social prediction for sport**, not football only. Football stays the first and main sport, and the heart of this chapter; the NFL is already live, and the model works for any sport with an official result. Public copy (site title, About, How Rivaly works, share images) says "sport".

## 1.1 Why Rivaly Exists

Football has always been about opinions.

Before every match, friends argue over who will win, defend their clubs, predict scores, and back their confidence with money. Those conversations are passionate, personal, and social.

Yet today's products separate these experiences:
- Sportsbooks ask users to bet against the house.
- Prediction markets are designed for traders rather than everyday football fans.
- The banter happens on WhatsApp.
- The predictions happen somewhere else.
- The money flows to the platform instead of between the people who disagreed in the first place.

We believe there is a better way. Rivaly exists to give football fans the best way to turn their football knowledge into money by challenging real people, competing fairly, and experiencing every match together.

## 1.2 Mission

To build the world's best peer-to-peer prediction platform where football fans compete with each other, not against the house.

Not simply the biggest. Not simply the most profitable. **The best.**

## 1.3 Vision

To become the world's most exciting platform for competitive predictions.

Football is where we begin because no sport creates stronger opinions, rivalries, or emotions. Over time, Rivaly will expand anywhere passionate disagreement exists: Sports, Entertainment, Politics, Esports, Culture — anywhere people have conviction and are willing to back that conviction.

## 1.4 What Rivaly Is

Rivaly is a **Peer-to-Peer Social Prediction Platform**.

It allows anyone to create prediction rooms around live events where people with opposing opinions compete with real money in transparent, custom markets. Users don't predict against bookmakers — they predict against each other. Rivaly simply provides the infrastructure that holds funds securely, verifies outcomes, and settles rooms fairly.

## 1.5 What Rivaly Is Not

- Rivaly is **not** a sportsbook.
- Rivaly is **not** a casino.
- Rivaly is **not** a bookmaker.
- Rivaly is **not** a traditional prediction exchange.
- Rivaly is **not** another social network.

Predictions are the product. Money creates the stakes. The social experience makes every prediction unforgettable.

## 1.6 Our Foundation

Everything inside Rivaly is built on three pillars.

**Prediction** — Everything begins with conviction. Users arrive because they believe their football opinion is right.

**Money** — Money creates accountability. Every prediction becomes meaningful because something valuable is on the line.

**Experience** — Competition should feel alive. Predictions should be exciting. Winning should feel rewarding. Losing should make users want an immediate rematch. Every interaction should amplify football — not distract from it.

### The Rivaly Formula

```
Prediction × Money × Experience = Rivaly
```

Remove any one of these pillars and Rivaly stops being Rivaly.

## 1.7 Our Beliefs

- We believe football knowledge deserves to be rewarded.
- We believe people care more about beating another person than beating probabilities.
- We believe competing against friends is more exciting than competing against the house.
- We believe transparency builds trust.
- We believe money should reward conviction — not exploit it.
- We believe football is already entertaining; our job is to amplify every match.
- We believe every prediction should create a story worth remembering.

## 1.8 Our Promise

Every time someone opens Rivaly they should immediately find:
- A prediction worth taking.
- Someone worth challenging.
- An opportunity to earn.
- A football experience worth remembering.

Whether they win or lose, they should leave wanting another match.

## 1.9 Our Difference

- Sportsbooks ask: **Can you beat the house?**
- Prediction markets ask: **What is the probability?**
- Rivaly asks: **Who thinks differently than you?**

That single question changes everything. Predictions become conversations. Money becomes competition. Football becomes personal.

## 1.10 Our North Star

Build the world's best platform for making money from football opinions.

Every feature. Every animation. Every interaction. Every screen. Every product decision. Must strengthen at least one of Rivaly's three pillars: **Prediction, Money, Experience**. The best decisions strengthen all three.
