# Rivaly First Principles

These ten principles are the fastest gut-check for any product decision. Each has a one-line test question — when in doubt, ask it.

### #1 — Football Comes First
Football is already one of the most emotional forms of entertainment in the world. Rivaly's job is never to compete with the match, but to amplify every goal, every prediction, every celebration, and every heartbreak.
> **Test:** Does this feature make watching football more exciting?

### #2 — People Want to Beat People
People don't dream about beating algorithms or sportsbooks — they dream about proving they're right. Rivaly exists to turn football opinions into real competition between real people.
> **Test:** Does this feature strengthen rivalry between users?

### #3 — Predictions Are the Product
Predictions are the heart of Rivaly. Everything else exists to make predicting more rewarding, more transparent, and more enjoyable. If the prediction experience doesn't improve, the feature probably doesn't belong.
> **Test:** Does this improve the prediction experience?

### #4 — Money Creates Stakes, Not Meaning
Winning money makes every prediction more exciting, but money is not the entire experience. Rivaly exists because the emotions surrounding the prediction — confidence, banter, victory, revenge, reputation — are just as valuable as the payout itself.
> **Test:** Does this make winning feel more meaningful rather than simply paying more?

### #5 — Trust Must Be Visible
Users should never have to wonder how a room was settled or where their money went. Every prediction, every settlement, and every payout should feel transparent, verifiable, and fair.
> **Test:** Can users clearly see why this outcome happened?

### #6 — Rivaly Never Bets Against Its Users
Rivaly is not the opponent. Rivaly never profits from users losing. We simply provide the arena where rivals compete while securely holding funds and settling outcomes fairly.
> **Test:** Does this reinforce that Rivaly is a trusted referee rather than the house?

### #7 — Every Feature Must Earn Its Place
Complexity is the enemy of great products. A feature should only exist if it strengthens one of three things: **Predictions, Rivalry, Social**. Everything else waits.
> **Test:** What core experience does this improve?

### #8 — Every Prediction Should Feel Personal
The best prediction is not an anonymous wager with strangers. It's challenging your friend, your rival, your favorite creator, or someone whose opinion matters to you.
> **Test:** Does this make predictions feel more personal?

### #9 — Every Match Should Create a Story
A football match shouldn't end with a payout. It should create memories, rivalries, rematches, celebrations, heartbreaks, and conversations that continue long after the final whistle.
> **Test:** What story does this feature help users create?

### #10 — Great Products Grow Through People
Every room should naturally encourage users to invite someone else. Rivaly grows because football is social by nature, not because users are forced to share.
> **Test:** Does this make someone want to bring another rival into the experience?
